Frequently Asked Questions

Are these compatible with standard Lego bricks? +

Yes, 100%. All PickMyBuild bricks, axles, pins, and gear teeth are engineered to standard international clutch pitch specifications (0.005mm tolerance) and interface seamlessly with all major brick brands.

What happens if a piece is missing in my box? +

We have a Zero Missing Parts Pledge. Simply take a photo of the manual step where the piece is required and message our builder WhatsApp helpline. We dispatch the exact replacement brick via express air courier within 24 hours at zero cost to you.

Intermediate kits (1,300 - 1,450 pcs) take approximately 3 to 4 hours. Advanced kits (1,500 - 1,750 pcs) take 5 to 7 hours. The Master edition Koenigsegg One:1 (1,980 pcs) takes 7 to 9 hours of rewarding assembly.
